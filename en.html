FAQ

 

Are these documents legally binding?

The documents follow standard drafting conventions, but this tool is a document generator — not legal advice and not attorney drafting. Legal effect depends on content, signatures and jurisdiction. For consequential matters, have a professional review the output.

How do I receive my document after paying?

Payment (via Gumroad) issues a license key instantly by email. Enter it in the Studio to unlock clean output — print, save as PDF, or download as text.

Do you store or see what I write?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser. We have no server that receives your document content — we can't see it even if we wanted to.

Can I write in Korean and output in other languages?

Yes. The form UI is currently Korean-first, but the generated document follows the conventions of whichever output language you select — including Japanese 通知書/警告書, Chinese 催告函/警告函, German Abmahnung/Mahnung/Kündigung, French mise en demeure/résiliation, Spanish requerimiento, Japanese 退職願/示談書, Russian претензия, Brazilian procuração, Arabic RTL output and more.

What is your refund policy?

Because the output is digital, refunds aren't available after the unlock code has been used. Unused purchases can be refunded within 7 days. See the legal page for details.
