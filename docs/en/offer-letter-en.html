September 14, 2026 Chris Johnson 88 Pine St, Jersey City, NJ Dear Chris Johnson, We are pleased to offer you the position of Senior Product Manager (Product — reporting to the CEO) at Omni Legal Inc., on the following terms: Start date October 5, 2026 Compensation $135,000 per year, paid bi-weekly Employment type full-time Benefits Health, dental and vision insurance 401(k) with 4% match 20 days PTO This offer is contingent upon: Satisfactory background check; Verification of work authorization. Your employment with Omni Legal Inc. will be at-will, meaning either you or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause or notice, except as otherwise provided by law. This letter is not a contract guaranteeing employment for any specific duration. Please sign and return this letter by 5 business days from the date above to accept this offer. We look forward to welcoming you to the team. Sincerely, Omni Legal Inc. ________________ Accepted: Chris Johnson ________________ Date: ____________

When you need this document

  • Extending a formal written offer before the full employment agreement

Drafting tips

  • State salary, start date and contingencies clearly — ambiguity here causes rescinded-offer disputes
  • Include at-will language for US employment
  • Set an expiration so the offer does not stay open indefinitely
⚠️ In most states an offer letter can create enforceable obligations — do not promise terms you may not honor.

FAQ

Offer letter vs employment contract?

The offer letter summarizes key terms; the full contract governs details. They should not contradict each other.
