When you need this document

  • Hiring or working as a freelancer for development, design, consulting or other services

Drafting tips

  • Define scope measurably — 'X deliverables, Y revision rounds' prevents the most common disputes
  • Always settle IP assignment — without a clause, work product stays with the creator
  • Use milestone or partial payments to align incentives
⚠️ If the working relationship involves fixed hours and direct supervision, it may legally be employment regardless of the contract title.
