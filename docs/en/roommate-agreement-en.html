ROOMMATE AGREEMENT PROPERTY 456 Maple Ave, Apt 7B, Queens, NY 11101 ROOMMATES Alice Kim Brian Lee Carol Park MONTHLY RENT $3,000 The roommates named above agree to share the rental property described above on the following terms. This agreement binds the roommates only and does not modify the underlying lease. 1. House Rules Kitchen cleaned weekly by rotation Trash taken out daily Quiet hours 11pm-7am 3. Move-out A roommate intending to move out will give the other roommates at least 30 days' written notice. The departing roommate remains responsible for their share of costs until a replacement is found or the notice period ends, whichever the roommates agree in writing. September 14, 2026 Signatures: ________________

When you need this document

  • Sharing a rental with roommates who may or may not all be on the lease

Drafting tips

  • Everyone named should sign — verbal splits cause the most disputes
  • Specify who is on the lease vs. who is a subtenant
  • Write the move-out notice period explicitly
⚠️ This agreement binds only the roommates. The landlord can still hold all leaseholders jointly liable regardless of internal splits.
